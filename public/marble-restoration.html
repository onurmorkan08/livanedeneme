Marble Restoration in Miami and South Florida

Service guide

What Livane Renovation provides

Livane Renovation restores worn and etched marble floors across South Florida through condition-led honing, polishing and detailing. Many marble floors can be restored when the stone assembly remains serviceable. Restoration commonly uses progressive honing to reduce etching, scratches and uneven wear, followed by polishing to develop the selected level of clarity. Deep damage, staining, lippage and previous repairs can limit the result and should be evaluated before work.

When this service is appropriate

  • Etching or dull traffic lanes
  • Fine scratches and uneven reflection
  • Inconsistent finish after prior spot repairs
  • Residue or coatings that obscure the stone
  • Edges and field areas that no longer match

Where the service is provided

Livane Renovation provides marble restoration project support across Miami-Dade County, Broward County and Palm Beach County as part of its South Florida service area.

Typical project conditions

Marble restoration in South Florida often takes place in occupied residences, condo lobbies and managed buildings. Protection routes, elevator scheduling, noise and slurry or dust controls should be built into the scope.

Material and surface expertise

Honed marble

A lower-sheen finish can reduce visible reflection and may suit some interiors better than high gloss.

Polished marble

Polishing develops clarity after proper refinement; it does not replace the corrective honing steps needed for etching.

Repairs and fills

Color, veining and age mean repairs may remain visible even when structurally serviceable.

Sealers

A compatible penetrating sealer can help with absorption but does not prevent acid etching.

Decision support

Honing or polishing?

Honing corrects and refines the surface; polishing develops clarity after the surface is sufficiently even. Polishing alone may not remove etching.

Restore or replace marble?

Restoration is often appropriate for serviceable stone with surface wear. Loose assemblies, major movement or unacceptable material limitations may change that decision.

Can sealing stop etching?

No. Sealers can reduce absorption of some liquids, but marble remains sensitive to acidic contact.

What affects scope and pricing

No responsible scope can be priced from the service name alone. The principal project variables are:

  • Area and room geometry
  • Etch and scratch severity
  • Lippage and edge access
  • Repairs or previous fills
  • Coatings and residues
  • Desired sheen
  • Protection and access
  • Furniture and phasing

Frequently asked questions

Can etched marble be polished?

Usually, but etching often requires honing before polishing because the surface itself has been chemically altered.

Will restoration make old marble look new?

It can greatly improve clarity and uniformity, but natural variation, deep stains and historic repairs may remain.

How long does marble restoration take?

Timing depends on area, condition, repairs, access and finish level; a condition review is required for scheduling.

Is marble restoration dusty?

The control method depends on the process. Protection and dust or slurry management should be defined before work starts.

How should restored marble be maintained?

Use stone-compatible products, prompt spill response and a maintenance method suited to the selected finish.

Can one room be restored alone?

Yes, though transitions to untreated areas and differences in sheen should be considered.

Prepare a useful scope review

Share clear photos, approximate square footage, the property location, current condition and desired result. That information helps separate a preliminary conversation from work that needs a site review.

About The Service

Commercial marble restoration in Miami is a specialized mechanical process required to correct severe etching, deep scratching, and overall dulling on high-end natural stone surfaces. In Miami's luxury hospitality, high-end retail, and corporate sectors, marble floors must maintain a flawless presentation, requiring contractor-level expertise to restore the stone without causing further damage.

We utilize wet diamond grinding and honing techniques to carefully and systematically remove the damaged microscopic top layer of the marble. This intense process physically levels the stone—completely removing uneven lippage—and eliminates deeply embedded stains. We never rely on topical waxes or artificial glosses, which degrade rapidly and look unprofessional in demanding environments.

Proper restoration re-establishes the natural density, clarity, and structural integrity of the marble. For South Florida property owners and managers overseeing premium real estate, our mechanical restoration process ensures that natural stone assets retain their slip resistance and high-value aesthetic under the strain of heavy daily foot traffic.

Our marble restoration protocols align closely with our broader floor polishing services, prioritizing mechanical refinement over chemical shortcuts. Whether we are dealing with travertine, limestone, or classic Carrara marble, we utilize condition-specific abrasives to achieve a true, natural polish that enhances the inherent beauty of the stone.

Routine maintenance cannot fix scratched or etched marble. By mechanically flattening and honing the stone, we eliminate the micro-abrasions that trap dirt. This results in a highly reflective, easily cleanable surface that significantly reduces the daily labor required by your in-house janitorial teams.

What This Service Includes

Lippage removal (flattening of uneven tiles), wet diamond honing to remove scratches and acidic etch marks, natural powder polishing using friction compounds, and the application of a premium penetrating stone sealer.

Where It Is Used

Luxury hotel lobbies, corporate headquarters, high-end retail boutiques, premium residential building common areas, commercial banking halls, and upscale restaurant dining rooms.

Process Overview

The marble is aggressively ground flat to eliminate uneven tile edges and deep damage. We then systematically hone the stone using progressively finer diamond abrasives to smooth the surface. Finally, the marble is polished using natural friction compounds and sealed to protect against future liquid penetration and staining.

Why It Matters

Damaged marble looks incredibly unprofessional and actively holds onto dirt and grime. Mechanical restoration brings the stone back to its original factory finish, providing a durable, naturally reflective surface that protects the property's high-end image and significantly lowers maintenance difficulty.

Our Approach

We evaluate the specific mineral composition and existing damage of your stone before proposing a solution. Our work is entirely condition-based, ensuring we use the exact weight, speed, and diamond grit sequence necessary to genuinely restore the marble without relying on temporary, artificial shine products.
