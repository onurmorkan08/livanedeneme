Company facts

What is Livane Renovation?

Livane Renovation is a South Florida renovation and surface restoration company founded by Onur Morkan and serving Miami-Dade, Broward and Palm Beach counties.

The company focuses on tile installation, kitchen remodeling, bathroom remodeling, patio renovation, floor restoration, marble and terrazzo restoration, concrete polishing and selected interior finish work. Typical projects include residences, occupied properties, condo and managed-property interiors, offices, retail environments and selected commercial spaces.

Projects begin with a review of current conditions, photographs, approximate area, access, adjoining finishes, material requirements and the intended result. Straightforward work may proceed from a documented scope review; complex or multi-scope work may require an onsite evaluation. Public project evidence is available in the project knowledge base, and the founder profile is available on the Onur Morkan page.

What We Stand For

How we work hasn't changed since that first lobby.

We still show up with first-day energy. We still walk the surface before we touch anything. We still don't leave until the result is something we'd be proud to hand back if it were our own building.

01

Evaluation Before Method

What a floor in Miami needs isn't always what a floor in Fort Lauderdale needs. What a marble lobby needs isn't what a concrete warehouse needs. We assess the actual surface condition before deciding anything — because the wrong process on the right surface is still the wrong answer.

02

The Quiet and Right Thing

We don't do the fast and careless thing. We do the quiet and right thing. Our crew doesn't rush to hit numbers. They move at the pace the work requires — methodical, focused, aware that the result lives in the building long after we're gone.

03

One Partner, Not a Vendor List

When a property needs floor restoration, interior finish work, and exterior surface sealing, we handle all of it under one coordinated scope. Fewer handoffs, cleaner scheduling, and a single point of accountability for the result.

04

Scope Clarity Before Mobilizing

We define the exact scope, surface access requirements, material specifications, and timeline before our crew sets foot on site. No surprises mid-project. No vague estimates that expand later. Owners, managers, and builders get exactly what they were told they would get.

05

Territory We Know Well

South Florida is our market. Miami-Dade, Fort Lauderdale, Broward County — we understand the climate conditions, the surface types common to the region, the pace of property management here, and how to coordinate within occupied spaces without disrupting tenants.

06

No Overcommitting

We don't pretend to be a full general contractor. We don't take work we can't execute well. We define our scope clearly and execute it with precision. That honesty is what keeps property owners and managers coming back — because in this industry, knowing what someone won't do is as valuable as knowing what they will.

How We Work

One small thing that keeps everything reliable.

Our process is designed so homeowners, property owners, managers, and builders can hand us a scope and step back — knowing the result will be what they were told it would be.

Before any crew mobilizes, we confirm the full scope: surface condition, access instructions, building rules, water hookup location, tenant schedule constraints, and anything else that affects how the work gets done. It's a human step that removes the kind of surprises that turn good renovation work into bad experiences.

"The job is not just the surface. The job is returning a better-looking, better-protected space to a client who can trust the work is handled."
01

Share the Scope

Send us photos, square footage, and a description of the surface condition. We review before committing to anything — because an honest scope starts with honest information.

02

Site Visit If Needed

For multi-scope or complex projects, we visit the property to assess conditions directly, measure accurately, and confirm the right method before providing pricing.

03

Scope & Timeline Agreement

We agree on the exact scope, materials, access requirements, and schedule in writing. No expansions mid-project. No vague language that shifts after you say yes.

04

Execution & Delivery

Our trained crew executes the work with professional-grade equipment. Site is left clean. The result is documented and delivered exactly as defined — and you don't have to chase us down to find out if it's done.

Why we care about this work.

The lobby terrazzo your building's had for forty years tells people something about you every single day. Whether it's been restored or neglected is obvious before anyone even looks up.

That's what we're here for.
