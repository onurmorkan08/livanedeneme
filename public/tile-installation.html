Service guide

What Livane Renovation provides

Livane Renovation provides tile installation across Miami-Dade, Broward and Palm Beach for residential renovations, managed properties and selected commercial interiors. Professional tile installation is more than placing tile in a pattern. The scope begins with the condition and flatness of the substrate, then accounts for layout, transitions, movement accommodation, setting materials, grout and edge details. The correct assembly varies between dry interiors, wet areas and exterior surfaces, so existing conditions must be reviewed before the installation method is defined.

When this service is appropriate

  • Cracked, loose or hollow-sounding existing tile
  • A finish change during a kitchen, bathroom or patio renovation
  • Uneven transitions between adjoining floor materials
  • Water-damaged finishes after the source of moisture has been resolved
  • A new substrate that requires a durable finished surface

South Florida tile projects often involve occupied condos, elevator reservations, delivery windows and building protection rules. Patio and exterior work also faces rain, heat and moisture exposure. These conditions affect sequencing and access even when the visible tile selection is unchanged.

Large-format tile

Larger units make substrate flatness, handling, coverage and lippage control especially important.

Interior and exterior assemblies

Exterior exposure and wet areas need different preparation and movement planning from conditioned interior rooms.

Decision support

Can tile be installed over existing tile?

Sometimes, but only when the existing assembly is well bonded, sufficiently flat and compatible with the proposed system. Added height, transitions and concealed failures often make removal the better choice.

Why does substrate condition matter?

Tile is a rigid finish and does not correct movement, major unevenness or an unstable base. Preparation is part of the installation, not a cosmetic extra.

Is large-format tile installed the same way?

No. Larger tile generally demands tighter flatness tolerances, deliberate handling and careful mortar coverage and alignment.

Frequently asked questions

How long does tile installation take?

Duration depends on area, removal, preparation, tile format, layout complexity and cure times. A small prepared room and a large occupied-property project should not share the same schedule assumption.

Does every old tile floor need removal?

No, but retaining it requires confirmation that it is stable, properly bonded and suitable for the new assembly.

Who supplies the tile?

Material procurement can be defined during scoping. The exact tile should be known before final layout, quantities and setting requirements are confirmed.

Can tile installation be coordinated with a remodel?

Yes. Tile should be sequenced with demolition, cabinetry, wet-area work, doors and other finishes to avoid rework.

Do you install exterior tile?

Selected patio and exterior scopes can be evaluated, with exposure, drainage, substrate and movement conditions considered separately from interior work.
