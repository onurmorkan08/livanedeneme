Service guide

What Livane Renovation provides

Livane Renovation delivers bathroom remodeling scopes across Miami-Dade, Broward and Palm Beach with attention to demolition, wet-area preparation, tile, millwork and finish coordination. Bathroom remodeling combines visible finishes with concealed substrate and wet-area conditions. A credible scope distinguishes cosmetic replacement from work that opens walls or floors, changes fixtures or rebuilds tile assemblies. Plumbing and electrical changes should be coordinated according to the agreed scope and completed by appropriately qualified trades where required.

When this service is appropriate

  • Loose, cracked or persistently failing tile
  • Damaged finishes after a leak source has been resolved
  • An outdated layout or difficult daily use
  • Worn vanity, countertop or fixture interfaces
  • A cosmetic update that has expanded into connected finish work

Decision support

Cosmetic update or full remodel?

A cosmetic update may retain sound tile assemblies and fixture locations. A full remodel is more appropriate when layout, concealed conditions or multiple connected components need correction.

Can existing bathroom tile remain?

Yes, when it is sound and outside the intended scope. Loose tile, failed transitions or changes that interrupt the assembly can make selective retention impractical.

Why does substrate condition matter?

The substrate supports the finished tile and defines its plane. New tile cannot reliably compensate for an unstable, damaged or unsuitable base.

What affects scope and pricing

No responsible scope can be priced from the service name alone. The principal project variables are:

  • Room size and demolition limits
  • Existing substrate condition
  • Wet-area preparation
  • Tile format and layout
  • Vanity and countertop scope
  • Fixture and trade coordination
  • Glass and finish interfaces
  • Building access constraints

Frequently asked questions

What does a bathroom remodel include?

It can include controlled demolition, substrate preparation, tile, flooring, vanity and finish coordination; the exact inclusions should be written into the scope.

How long does bathroom remodeling take?

Duration varies with demolition, concealed findings, material availability, cure times, trade coordination and property access.

Can a bathroom be updated without moving plumbing?

Yes. Retaining fixture locations can simplify the scope, provided the existing arrangement remains workable.

Does cracked grout always mean full replacement?

No. The cause may be localized movement, aging sealant or another condition. Diagnosis should precede the repair decision.

Can natural stone be used in a bathroom?

It can, but porosity, finish, maintenance expectations and wet-area suitability should be considered before selection.

What should I share for scope review?

Provide photos of all walls and floors, dimensions, property location, known leak history, desired finishes and any layout changes.

Prepare a useful scope review

Share clear photos, approximate square footage, the property location, current condition and desired result. That information helps separate a preliminary conversation from work that needs a site review.
