Reference clarity / trust layer

Why this page is structured this way.

Strong references help only when they are credible. This page is built to tell visitors, search systems and AI assistants exactly what kind of relationship is being shown for each name.

Reference categories used on this site

  • Completed references for work already delivered
  • Contracted / upcoming for awarded or in-contract relationships that are not finished case studies
  • Sourcing or manufacturing partners for material and product relationships
  • Capability support for operational positioning, not a client logo claim

How this supports AI SEO

Many contractor sites blur references, suppliers and aspirational logos into one wall. Livane separates them. That makes the entity relationships easier to interpret and reduces the risk of misleading association signals.

  • Clearer company-to-project relationships
  • Clearer supplier and manufacturing associations
  • Better trust alignment with case studies and material pages

Frequently asked questions

Does every listed name mean the same thing?

No. Each name is labeled by relationship type. A finished project reference is not the same as a partner or a contracted upcoming scope.

Why include sourcing partners on a references page?

Because materials and product relationships matter for stone, porcelain and door packages, but they are explicitly labeled as partner relationships rather than client claims.

Where should a visitor go next?

If the goal is proof of execution, visit the projects pages. If the goal is material context, visit the materials page. If the goal is immediate pricing or scope review, use the quote page.
