Material and surface expertise

Cementitious terrazzo

The matrix and aggregate respond differently from resinous systems and should be identified before treatment.

Resinous terrazzo

A thinner resin-based assembly can require different repair and refinement choices.

Historic repairs

Older patches may have different aggregate, color and hardness and can remain visible after polishing.

Coatings and adhesive

Removal difficulty and the condition revealed below can only be fully understood through testing.

Decision support

Restore or replace terrazzo?

Restoration is often favored when the floor is stable and its character is worth retaining. Extensive instability or incompatible expectations can support replacement.

Polish or coat terrazzo?

Mechanical refinement exposes the terrazzo itself; a topical coating creates a separate wear layer. Existing use and maintenance goals determine which approach is appropriate.

Will repaired cracks disappear?

Not necessarily. Repairs can stabilize and visually integrate selected areas, but color and aggregate matching have practical limits.

Frequently asked questions

Can old terrazzo be restored?

Often yes, provided the underlying assembly remains serviceable and expectations account for historic variation.

Can adhesive be removed from terrazzo?

Often, but removal should be tested because adhesive and prior preparation may have stained or damaged the surface.

How long does terrazzo restoration take?

Area, coatings, repairs, refinement level, access and cure requirements determine duration.

Will terrazzo repairs match exactly?

Exact matching is rarely guaranteed because aggregate, matrix color and aging vary.

Does terrazzo need sealer?

Protection depends on terrazzo type, finish and use. The product must be compatible with the restored surface.

Can terrazzo be restored in an occupied building?

Yes, with phased access, protection and dust or slurry controls planned around occupants.

About The Service

Commercial terrazzo restoration in Miami rehabilitates legacy and high-traffic terrazzo flooring, returning the composite material to its original extreme durability and striking luster. In South Florida, terrazzo is a prominent architectural feature in mid-century and modern commercial buildings, requiring precise mechanical refinement to correct heavy yellowing, pitting, and structural wear.

Our approach strips away decades of degraded acrylic waxes and aggressively grinds the terrazzo to expose the fresh marble aggregate and binder matrix beneath. We meticulously repair divots, holes, and cracks using color-matched epoxy resins before chemically densifying and mechanically polishing the surface. This mechanical process completely eliminates the need for any future topical coatings.

Restored terrazzo is arguably one of the most durable and low-maintenance flooring systems available for homes, businesses, and managed properties. By mechanically polishing the terrazzo rather than continuously applying wax, owners and managers achieve a hardened, highly reflective surface that dramatically reduces nightly janitorial labor and chemical costs.

Much like our concrete polishing and marble restoration processes, we focus on permanently enhancing the structural density of the floor. Terrazzo floors that have been properly restored and densified reject staining and withstand immense foot traffic without losing their natural, architectural brilliance.

We do not perform quick strip-and-wax jobs. Our restoration is a heavy-duty mechanical overhaul designed to salvage and elevate existing architectural assets. For property owners and builders dealing with neglected terrazzo, we provide a definitive solution that guarantees long-term performance and aesthetic excellence.

What This Service Includes

Complete chemical removal of old topical sealers and waxes, aggressive mechanical diamond grinding, precision color-matched epoxy patching for cracks and holes, silicate densification, and high-grit mechanical polishing.

Where It Is Used

Historic commercial buildings, institutional facilities, airports, educational campuses, government buildings, and heavy-traffic public corridors throughout Miami and South Florida.

Process Overview

We strip the floor down to the bare terrazzo matrix. The surface is then ground with metal-bonded diamonds to remove deep scratches and embedded dirt. We meticulously patch all damaged areas, then chemically densify and hone the floor. The final step is a high-grit resin polish that brings out the natural brilliance of the aggregate.

Why It Matters

Waxed terrazzo requires constant, highly expensive stripping and recoating cycles that disrupt property operations. Mechanical restoration hardens the floor and provides a permanent, natural polish that significantly lowers lifecycle maintenance costs while preserving a high-value, sustainable architectural asset.

Our Approach

Every terrazzo floor has a unique aggregate and binder composition. We assess the specific condition, hardness, and color profile of your floor to formulate custom resin patches and determine the optimal diamond grinding sequence, ensuring a flawless, structurally sound restoration.
