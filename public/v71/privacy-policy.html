Overview

Livane Renovation respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect through livanerenovation.com, why we collect it, and the choices available to you.

When you request a quote, contact us, or otherwise submit information through the website, we may collect your name, email address, phone number, project address, project details, and any other information you choose to provide.

We may also automatically receive limited technical information, such as your IP address, browser type, device information, referring pages, and interactions with the website through cookies or similar technologies.

Our website may use cookies and analytics tools, including Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager, to understand website usage and improve performance. You can limit cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some website features.

We do not sell your personal information. These providers are permitted to use information only for the services they perform for us.

We retain personal information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy or as required by law. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no method of online transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your Choices

You may ask to access, correct, or delete personal information you have submitted to us, or opt out of non-essential communications. Depending on where you live, additional privacy rights may apply.

The website may contain links to third-party websites. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies, and we are not responsible for their content or practices.

Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
