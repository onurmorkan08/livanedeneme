What Livane Renovation provides

Livane Renovation installs selected interior doors across South Florida with attention to opening dimensions, jamb conditions, floor clearance, hardware and adjoining finishes. Interior door installation depends on more than the door slab. The rough or finished opening, jamb, wall thickness, handing, hinge preparation, hardware, casing, floor height and required clearances must work together. Replacement in an existing opening can be more constrained than installation in properly prepared new construction.

Decision support

Slab replacement or prehung door?

A slab may work when the existing jamb is sound and accurately matched. A prehung unit is often more appropriate when the jamb is damaged or alignment must be reset.

Install doors before or after flooring?

Finished floor elevation must be known either way. Sequence depends on flooring type, jamb detail and protection plan.

Can an uneven opening be corrected by the door?

Only limited variation can be managed during setting. Significant framing or wall conditions require separate correction.

Frequently asked questions

Can only the door slab be replaced?

Yes, when the existing jamb, hinges and dimensions are suitable for a carefully matched slab.

What is door handing?

Handing describes the swing and hinge orientation and must be confirmed from the intended approach side.

Are doors installed before painting?

Sequence varies, but finish responsibility and protection should be coordinated before installation.

Can new flooring make a door bind?

Yes. Added floor height or transitions can reduce clearance and should be checked before ordering or cutting.

How long does installation take?

Door count, opening condition, frame type, hardware and finish work determine duration.

What information is needed?

Provide opening photos and dimensions, door type, handing if known, hardware, floor condition and property location.

Prepare a useful scope review

Share clear photos, approximate square footage, the property location, current condition and desired result. That information helps separate a preliminary conversation from work that needs a site review.
