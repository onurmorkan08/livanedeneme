The Problem

Too Many Vendors for Closely Related Work.

Renovation projects often require several related scopes — floor restoration, interior finish work, exterior surface treatment. When each scope goes to a separate vendor, the result is fragmented scheduling, misaligned sequencing, more coordination overhead, and accountability gaps.

Livane Renovation was built to solve that. We handle multiple related improvement scopes under one relationship — with a single point of contact, a unified schedule, and a clear definition of what each scope includes.

✕Multiple vendors with separate schedules that don't align
✕Accountability gaps when scopes overlap between contractors
✕Fragmented pricing that makes budgeting harder
✕Repeated site visits and handoff delays between companies
